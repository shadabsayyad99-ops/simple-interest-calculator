# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple application that calculates the simple interest and total amount based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest (SI) is calculated using the formula:

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

- P = Principal Amount
- R = Rate of Interest
- T = Time Period

Total Amount = Principal Amount + Simple Interest

## Input

The calculator takes the following inputs:

1. Principal Amount
2. Rate of Interest
3. Time Period

## Example

If:

- Principal Amount = ₹10,000
- Rate of Interest = 5%
- Time Period = 2 years

Then:

Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹10,000 + ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹11,000

## Features

- Calculates simple interest
- Calculates total amount
- Easy to use
- Simple and beginner-friendly

## Conclusion

The Simple Interest Calculator provides a quick and easy way to calculate simple interest and the total amount using the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.
